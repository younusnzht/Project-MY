    About Noor

    Grade 5 project: “Design an AI Robot.”

    🎯 Purpose

    Noor is a friendly helper robot for students. She listens to your voice, thinks about what you asked, and answers out loud. She helps with homework, explains new words, does maths, tells stories, and sets timers.

    👂 Sensors

    SensorStatusWhat it does
    🎤 MicrophoneWorking nowThe sound sensor. It hears your voice so Noor can turn it into words.
    📷 CameraProposed ideaWould let a physical Noor see faces and read homework.
    📏 Distance sensorProposed ideaWould tell Noor how far away a person or wall is, so she can turn to face you and not bump into things.
    ✋ Touch sensorProposed ideaWould let you tap Noor’s hand to say hello or wake her up.

    The proposed sensors are for an imagined physical robot. This laptop app only has the microphone (and the speaker for her voice).

    🧠 AI features

    • Speech recognition: turns your voice into text.
    • Conversational AI: reads the whole chat so Noor understands follow-up questions.
    • Memory: remembers your name during one conversation.
    • Speech synthesis: turns Noor’s words into a voice.
    • Maths and timers: done by exact rules in the app, so the answers are correct.

    🌟 Benefits

    • Patient: she never gets tired of questions.
    • Speaking and listening helps students who find typing hard.
    • Explains things in simple words, step by step.
    • Timers keep study time and breaks on track.

    ⚠️ Limitations

    • She can mishear. Background noise or fast talking can make her hear the wrong words. Check the “I heard” box.
    • She can be wrong. AI sometimes gives incorrect answers. Always check important facts with a teacher or a book.
    • Internet needed. The AI brain and Chrome’s speech recognition use the internet. Without it, Noor can only use Demo Mode and typing.
    • Timers only work while this page stays open.
    • She has no body, camera, or touch sensors yet. Those are future ideas.
